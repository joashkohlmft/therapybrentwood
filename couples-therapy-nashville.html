Couples counseling for Nashville, without the commute

Joash Koh LMFT is an ICEEFT Triple-Certified EFT Therapist offering online couples counseling and marriage therapy for Nashville couples. No office visit required, sessions are private, secure, and available from anywhere in Tennessee.

Couples counseling and relationship therapy in Nashville, TN

Nashville is a city that demands a lot from the people who live here. Whether you work in healthcare at Vanderbilt or HCA, in the music industry, in one of the corporate offices that have helped make Nashville one of America's fastest-growing cities, or simply in the relentless rhythm of raising a family in a place that never stops moving, the pressure builds. And it often lands in your relationship first.

Many Nashville couples who seek relationship counseling describe a version of the same experience: they built something impressive together, careers, a home, a family, a life they can be proud of, and somewhere along the way, the connection that made it all worthwhile quietly eroded. They are excellent partners in logistics and strangers in intimacy.

Nashville also has a distinctive quality that affects couples more than most cities acknowledge: it is a city of transplants. Many couples here have no extended family nearby, no longstanding community roots, and no built-in support network. When the relationship is under strain, there is often no one to turn to. The distance between two people who are already each other's primary support becomes especially heavy.

Couples counseling and marriage therapy exist for exactly these moments, not to fix a failing relationship, but to rebuild the emotional bond that makes the whole thing feel worthwhile again.

Why Nashville couples choose online couples therapy

Nashville's traffic is not a small inconvenience. Anyone who has driven I-65 during a Predators game, or tried to get through Green Hills on a Friday afternoon, or navigated the I-24/I-40 interchange at the wrong time of day knows what it costs. For couples already stretched thin between work, parenting, and a city that never slows down, adding a 90-minute round trip to a couples therapy appointment is often what keeps people from starting at all.

Online couples counseling removes that barrier entirely.

Sessions take place via a secure, HIPAA-compliant video platform, private, professional, and accessible from anywhere in Tennessee. Both partners join from the same location or from separate devices. The research on this is clear: telehealth couples therapy produces outcomes equivalent to in-person work. The modality does not limit what is possible in the therapeutic relationship.

For Nashville couples, online therapy also offers something harder to quantify: discretion. There is no waiting room. No one sees your car in the parking lot. Therapy happens in the privacy of your own home, on a schedule that works around irregular hours, shift work, or a life that does not fit neatly into a 9-to-5 window.

What makes EFT the gold standard in couples counseling

Not all couples therapy, or couples counseling, or marriage therapy, or relationship counseling, is the same. The approach matters. The therapist's training matters. And in a city with as many therapists as Nashville has, that distinction is worth understanding.

I practice Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT), the most extensively researched approach to couples work in the world. The APA has designated EFT as the gold standard in couples therapy research, a designation earned through 40 years of replicated clinical trials across multiple countries and populations, including Chinese-speaking couples in Canada, Spanish-speaking couples across Latin America and Spain, and ongoing studies in Africa and Eastern Europe.

75% of couples achieve full recovery from relationship distress through EFT, among the highest rates recorded for any couples therapy modality (ICEEFT Research Summary). 86–90% show significant improvement. And critically: those gains hold. Research consistently shows EFT outcomes are sustained and continue to deepen at two-year follow-up.

EFT does not work primarily through communication exercises or behavioral homework. It works at the level of emotional attachment, the deep relational bond that shapes how we feel safe, seen, and loved. In an EFT session, we explore, with curiosity, beneath the surface of conflict. We identify the negative cycle both partners are caught in, the pursuer-withdrawer dynamic, the emotional shutdown, the reactive moves that leave everyone feeling worse, and we work to understand what is actually driving it. Usually, it is fear. Fear of rejection. Fear of not mattering. Fear of being too much, or not enough.

When those underlying emotions are accessed and expressed safely, something shifts. Partners begin to hear each other differently. Defenses soften. The cycle changes. The bond deepens.

Triple certification, unique in the Nashville area

I am ICEEFT Triple-Certified in EFT for couples (EFCT), individuals (EFIT), and families (EFFT), a designation held by fewer than several hundred therapists worldwide. I am also an EFT Supervisor Candidate, supervising other therapists in the EFT model. I am not aware of another couples counselor or marriage therapist currently serving the Nashville/Brentwood area who holds triple certification from ICEEFT.

Certification is not a marketing term. ICEEFT certification requires hundreds of supervised clinical hours, video review of actual therapy sessions by certified trainers, and demonstrated competence at each stage of the EFT model. It is the difference between a therapist who has read about EFT and one who has been trained and evaluated in it.

How couples counseling works in practice

We begin with a complimentary 20-minute consultation, no pressure, no commitment. Just an honest conversation about where you are and what you need. If we are a good fit, we move forward together.

Sessions are typically 50 minutes, held weekly or bi-weekly via secure video. In the early sessions, I focus on understanding your specific cycle, the recurring pattern of moves and countermoves that leaves both of you feeling worse. I name it, help you both see it, and externalize it so the cycle becomes the shared problem rather than each other. Most couples find this reframe brings immediate relief even in early sessions.

As therapy progresses, we go deeper, accessing the fears and longings underneath the reactive behaviors. Partners who have felt invisible begin to be seen. Partners who have shut down begin to open up. The relationship changes from the inside out.

Most couples begin to feel meaningful shifts within 8–12 sessions. Couples facing an acute crisis, an affair, a major rupture, years of accumulated distance, often benefit from a therapy intensive: a concentrated multi-day format that compresses months of progress into a single focused experience.

Common questions

Do you offer couples counseling for Nashville couples?

Yes. I offer online couples counseling for Nashville couples throughout Davidson County and across Tennessee via secure telehealth. No office visit is required. Sessions are conducted via a private, HIPAA-compliant video platform and are accessible from anywhere in the state.

How does online couples therapy work for Nashville couples?

Both partners join a secure video session from the same location or from separate devices. Sessions are 50 minutes, held weekly or bi-weekly. Research consistently shows that telehealth couples therapy produces outcomes equivalent to in-person work, the modality does not limit what is possible in the therapeutic relationship.

Is online therapy as effective as in-person therapy?

Yes. Multiple peer-reviewed studies have found that telehealth therapy produces outcomes equivalent to in-person therapy across a wide range of presenting concerns, including couples work. For many Nashville couples, online therapy is actually more accessible, no commute, flexible scheduling around irregular work hours, and sessions conducted in the privacy of your own home.

What is the difference between couples counseling and couples therapy?

The terms are widely used interchangeably and refer, in practice, to the same thing: professional support for couples navigating relationship distress. I practice Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT), a structured, evidence-based approach that works at the level of emotional attachment rather than surface behavior, producing deeper and more lasting change than skills-based approaches alone.

How is EFT different from Gottman Method couples therapy?

Both EFT and Gottman are evidence-based. EFT focuses on restructuring the emotional bond itself, helping couples understand and change the attachment patterns driving conflict. Gottman focuses on building friendship, managing conflict, and creating shared meaning through practical skills-based tools. EFT has the stronger recovery research: 75% full recovery from relationship distress, with gains that continue growing at two-year follow-up. See the full comparison on the EFT vs. Gottman page.

How many couples counseling sessions will we need?

Most Nashville couples begin to feel meaningful shifts within 8–12 sessions. The depth and pace vary by couple. Those facing acute crises, infidelity, a major rupture, years of accumulated distance, often benefit from a therapy intensive: a concentrated multi-day format that compresses months of progress into a single focused experience.
