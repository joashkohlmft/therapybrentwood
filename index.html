About Joash

Brentwood's EFT-certified marriage & family therapist

I'm Joash Koh — a Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist, EFT-certified therapist, and former telecommunications RF engineer and consultant who worked with leading global telecoms companies across Asia and beyond.

That background matters. I understand the analytical mind, the pressure of high-stakes careers, and the particular way that professional success can quietly hollow out a relationship. I've lived in that world — and I bring that understanding into the therapy room alongside my deep clinical training.

I don't do passive therapy. My approach is active, strategic, and designed for people who value clear communication and measurable progress.

Who I work with

Therapy for people who hold everything together — except each other

My clients are often high-achieving professionals — engineers, executives, physicians, entrepreneurs, lawyers — who are exceptional at managing complexity at work and struggling to translate that competence into their closest relationship.

They are used to solving problems analytically. They have tried talking it through, reading the books, taking the vacations. And yet the distance keeps growing. Or the same fight keeps happening. Or one of them has stopped trying altogether.

What they need is not more communication techniques. They need to understand what is happening at an emotional level — the fears, the unmet needs, the attachment injuries that sit beneath the surface of every argument — and they need a therapist who can go there with them without losing the clinical rigor they expect.

You may recognize this

  • · You excel at work and feel like strangers at home
  • · Success came at the cost of your relationship
  • · You analyze the problem but can't feel your way through it
  • · You've tried therapy before and it felt too surface-level
  • · You want a therapist who can keep up with you
  • · You need a peer who understands the unique tax that ambition places on a marriage
  • · Discretion and confidentiality are non-negotiable
