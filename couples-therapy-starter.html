Why this works when talking it out hasn't

Most couples don't have a communication problem so much as a pattern problem. One of you reaches, the other pulls back, and the more that happens, the more stuck you both feel. Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT) is the most researched approach to couples work, and it targets exactly that pattern rather than just teaching you scripts to say.

My job isn't to take sides or referee your arguments. It's to help you both see the cycle you're caught in, slow it down, and rebuild the sense of safety that makes everything else possible.

Common questions

Is online couples therapy as effective as in person?

Yes. Research finds telehealth couples therapy works as well as in-office sessions for most couples, and many find it easier to be open from their own home, with no commute and no waiting room.

What if we're not sure therapy is even the right step?

That's common, and it's exactly what the starter is for. The three sessions give us both a clear read on what's happening and whether ongoing work makes sense, before you commit to anything longer.

How many sessions will we need after the starter?

It varies, but most couples doing focused EFT work see meaningful change over a few months of weekly sessions. After the starter I'll give you an honest sense of what your situation is likely to take.

Do you take insurance?

I'm private pay, which keeps your care confidential and on your terms. I can provide a superbill you may submit to your insurer for possible out-of-network reimbursement.
