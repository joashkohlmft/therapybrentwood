Parts Work & Family of Origin · Brentwood, TN · Online Sessions

The patterns you carry today began long before today

Parts work and family of origin therapy help you understand, and heal, the internal patterns, protective strategies, and early wounds that continue to shape your relationships, your emotions, and your sense of self.

What is parts work and family of origin therapy?

Parts work and family of origin therapy explore how our earliest relationships, with parents, caregivers, and family systems, shape the emotional patterns, beliefs, and behaviors we carry into adult life. By understanding and healing these internal dynamics, it becomes possible to change patterns that have persisted for years, in relationships and within yourself. I offer this work as part of Emotionally Focused Individual Therapy (EFIT) throughout Tennessee via telehealth.

Why we do what we do, even when we don't want to

Have you ever found yourself reacting in ways you don't fully understand? Shutting down when you want to stay open. Lashing out when you want to stay calm. Choosing the same kinds of relationships over and over. Feeling like there are different parts of you that want different things, and none of them are fully in charge?

These experiences often have roots in our earliest relationships, with our parents, our family systems, the emotional environments we grew up in. Our families taught us, often without words, what emotions were safe to feel, what needs were okay to have, and what we had to do to belong and survive.

Parts work and family of origin therapy help you trace these patterns back to their source, and begin to change them from the inside out.

What is parts work?

Parts work is a way of understanding the self not as a single unified entity but as a system of different inner parts, each with its own perspective, its own history, and its own way of trying to help you. Some parts protect you from pain. Some carry the wounds that were never fully healed. Some have been exiled because they felt too vulnerable or too much.

Drawing from both Internal Family Systems (IFS) and Emotionally Focused Individual Therapy (EFIT), I help you develop a compassionate, curious relationship with all these parts of yourself. When the most wounded parts finally feel seen and heard, rather than managed, suppressed, or criticized, something profound shifts.

You are not broken. The parts of you that seem to sabotage you or hold you back were formed as acts of survival. Parts work is about understanding them with compassion, and helping them find new ways to protect you that don't cost you so much.

What is family of origin therapy?

Our families of origin are the first place we learned about love, safety, boundaries, and belonging. Even loving families transmit patterns, anxious attachment, emotional unavailability, enmeshment, perfectionism, the unspoken rule that certain feelings aren't allowed.

Family of origin work helps you:

  • Understand the emotional patterns you inherited, not to blame your family, but to see clearly
  • Identify the beliefs about yourself and relationships that formed in childhood
  • Grieve what you didn't receive, the attunement, safety, or recognition you deserved
  • Differentiate, develop a clearer, more autonomous sense of who you are outside of your family system
  • Change the patterns you carry into your adult relationships

What can parts work and family of origin therapy address?

  • Inner critic and harsh self-judgment
  • People-pleasing and difficulty with boundaries
  • Fear of abandonment or engulfment in relationships
  • Shame and chronic unworthiness
  • Perfectionism and achievement-based self-worth
  • Anger, numbness, or emotional shutdown
  • Enmeshment or estrangement from family
  • Parentification, having been the emotional caretaker in your family
  • Intergenerational trauma and cultural wounds
  • Spiritual wounds from family religious environments

My approach: IFS and EFIT blended

I draw primarily from Internal Family Systems (IFS) and Emotionally Focused Individual Therapy (EFIT), two approaches that complement each other beautifully. IFS offers a detailed map of the inner system and a compassionate framework for working with parts. EFIT brings the depth of attachment theory and emotional processing, helping you access, experience, and transform the deeper feelings beneath the surface patterns.

Together, they allow for work that is both structured and deeply felt, honoring both your intellect and your emotional and embodied experience.

What can I expect from parts work therapy?

This kind of therapy moves differently from symptom-focused work. We are not trying to eliminate difficult feelings, we are trying to understand them. Early sessions focus on building a map of the parts of you that are most present in your daily life and relationships: the part that shuts down, the part that over-functions, the part that reaches and never feels satisfied.

As therapy deepens, we begin to work with the family of origin patterns underneath, not to assign blame, but to understand what your early relationships taught you about safety, love, and what you have to do to belong. Many clients describe this phase as finally understanding themselves in a way they never have before.

The work tends to be longer-term, most clients find it genuinely transformative over 3–12 months of consistent work. Individual sessions are 50 minutes, conducted online via a secure, HIPAA-compliant platform throughout Tennessee.

How does parts work and family of origin therapy work, phase by phase?

This work moves through recognizable phases, though the pace is always shaped by what is present and what each person needs:

1

Consultation: beginning the conversation

We start with a 20-minute consultation to talk about what brings you here and whether this approach feels right. You do not need a clear diagnosis or a tidy explanation of the problem, only a sense that something is getting in the way.

2

Mapping the inner system: meeting your parts

Early sessions focus on building a map of the parts most active in your daily life, the critic, the people-pleaser, the protector, the part that shuts down. We approach each part with curiosity rather than judgment, understanding what it is trying to do and what it fears would happen if it stopped.

3

Tracing patterns to their roots: family of origin work

As the inner system becomes clearer, we begin to trace where these parts came from, the emotional environment you grew up in, the messages you received about what feelings were safe, the roles you had to play to belong. This phase often involves grief: for what was missed, for the childhood self who adapted in order to survive. Many clients describe this as the first time they have truly understood themselves.

4

Integration: becoming more fully yourself

When the wounded parts have been heard and the protective parts no longer need to work so hard, a deeper coherence begins to emerge, a more settled, compassionate relationship with yourself, and more freedom in your closest relationships. This is differentiation: becoming distinctly and securely yourself, outside of your family system's script.

Common questions

What is parts work therapy?

Parts work therapy, drawing from Internal Family Systems (IFS) and related models, recognizes that we contain multiple inner parts or sub-personalities, each with their own feelings, beliefs, and protective roles. Therapy helps you access and understand these parts rather than fight them, creating internal coherence and healing.

What is family of origin work?

Family of origin work explores how your earliest relationships, with parents, siblings, and caregivers, shaped the attachment patterns, beliefs, and relational dynamics you carry into adult life. Understanding these roots helps explain why certain situations trigger intense reactions and why certain relational patterns repeat.

Do you offer parts work therapy in Brentwood or Nashville TN?

Yes. I offer parts work and family of origin therapy throughout Tennessee via telehealth, integrated within an EFT and attachment-informed framework.

What can parts work and family of origin therapy address?

Parts work and family of origin therapy can address inner critic and harsh self-judgment, people-pleasing and difficulty with boundaries, fear of abandonment or engulfment in relationships, shame and chronic unworthiness, perfectionism and achievement-based self-worth, anger or emotional shutdown, enmeshment or estrangement from family, parentification and over-responsibility, intergenerational trauma, cultural wounds, and spiritual wounds from family religious environments.

What does your parts work approach look like in practice?

The approach blends Internal Family Systems (IFS) and Emotionally Focused Individual Therapy (EFIT). IFS provides a detailed map of the inner system and a compassionate framework for working with protective and wounded parts. EFIT brings the depth of attachment theory and emotional processing, helping you access, experience, and transform the feelings beneath surface patterns. Together they allow for work that is both structured and genuinely transformative. Most clients find this work meaningful over a period of 3 to 12 months.
