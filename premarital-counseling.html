Premarital Counseling · Marriage Preparation · Brentwood, TN

Build the foundation your marriage deserves, before the wedding day

Premarital counseling isn't about fixing problems. It's about understanding each other deeply, navigating differences honestly, and entering marriage with eyes wide open and hearts fully prepared.

What is premarital counseling?

Premarital counseling is a structured process that helps couples build a strong foundation before marriage, identifying patterns, exploring differences, and developing tools for navigating the challenges every marriage eventually faces. I offer PREPARE/ENRICH-certified premarital counseling grounded in Emotionally Focused Therapy, available throughout Tennessee via telehealth.

Why does premarital counseling matter?

The early years of marriage are often the hardest, not because love fades, but because two people with different histories, communication styles, families, and expectations are trying to build a life together. Most couples spend more time planning their wedding than preparing for their marriage.

Premarital counseling gives you the conversations you might not know to have, and a framework for navigating the ones that will inevitably come up later.

Research consistently shows that couples who complete premarital counseling have significantly lower divorce rates and higher relationship satisfaction over time. It is one of the highest-return investments you can make in your future together.

What is the PREPARE/ENRICH assessment?

As a certified PREPARE/ENRICH facilitator and trainer, I use this gold-standard assessment tool with every premarital couple I work with. PREPARE/ENRICH is a research-based inventory used by couples and counselors worldwide, with over 4 million couples having completed it.

The assessment reveals your strengths as a couple, the areas where you are most likely to experience conflict, and your individual attachment and communication patterns. It gives us a detailed, personalized roadmap for our sessions together.

PREPARE/ENRICH is available for couples of all faiths and backgrounds, including non-religious, Christian, Buddhist, Muslim, and spiritual but not religious couples.

What do we explore in premarital counseling?

  • Communication styles and conflict patterns
  • Attachment styles and emotional needs
  • Family of origin, the invisible scripts you each carry
  • Finances, roles, and expectations about partnership
  • Children and parenting philosophies
  • Sexuality and physical intimacy
  • Faith, spirituality, and values
  • Cultural and family background differences
  • How to maintain connection through life's inevitable stresses

Who is premarital counseling for?

Premarital counseling is for any couple preparing for marriage, regardless of how long you've been together, your cultural or religious background, or whether this is a first or subsequent marriage. I am particularly attuned to multicultural couples, intercultural and interfaith partnerships, and couples navigating different immigration or family expectations.

I also work with couples who are engaged or seriously considering engagement and want to ensure they are making their decision with full clarity and awareness.

What can I expect from premarital counseling?

Most premarital counseling involves 4–8 sessions. We begin with the PREPARE/ENRICH assessment, a validated relationship inventory that gives us a personalized map of your relationship across 20 dimensions: communication, conflict resolution, finances, sexuality, family of origin expectations, and more.

Each subsequent session works through the areas the assessment surfaces, but in a way that feels like genuine conversation rather than clinical curriculum. You won't be handed worksheets. The sessions are warm, exploratory, and grounded in what actually matters to both of you.

By the time we finish, you will have had conversations most couples don't have until years into marriage, and you will have a shared language and framework for navigating the ones that come later. Sessions are conducted online via a secure, HIPAA-compliant platform throughout Tennessee.

How does premarital counseling work, step by step?

Premarital counseling with me follows a clear, structured process, but one that never feels clinical or transactional. Here is what to expect from beginning to end:

1

Complimentary consultation

We begin with a 20-minute conversation to get a sense of your goals, your relationship, and whether this approach is the right fit. No pressure, just an honest conversation about what you are hoping to build.

2

PREPARE/ENRICH assessment

Each partner completes the PREPARE/ENRICH inventory online, approximately 30–45 minutes. The assessment covers 20 relationship dimensions: communication, conflict, finances, sexuality, spirituality, family of origin, and more. The results give us a personalized map of where you are strong and where you will benefit most from focused attention.

3

Feedback session: understanding your results

In our first full session, we review the assessment results together. This often surfaces patterns and differences that couples had sensed but never put into words, and gives us a clear, shared agenda for the work ahead.

4

Sessions working through what matters most

Most premarital counseling involves 4–8 sessions total. Each session is guided by the assessment results and what emerges in our conversations, covering the topics most relevant to your relationship. Sessions feel like genuine, warm conversations rather than worksheets or curriculum. You leave each one with greater clarity and a shared language.

5

A shared foundation for what comes next

By the end of premarital counseling, you will have had conversations most couples don't have until years into marriage. Many couples return for occasional sessions as their relationship evolves, an intensive, a check-in before a major transition, or ongoing work when life gets hard.

Common questions

What does premarital counseling include?

Premarital counseling with me includes the PREPARE/ENRICH assessment, one of the most research-validated premarital tools available, alongside EFT-informed exploration of each partner's attachment patterns, family of origin dynamics, communication styles, and the foundations that will sustain the relationship through the inevitable pressures of married life.

When should couples start premarital counseling?

Ideally 6 to 12 months before the wedding, though couples at any stage of engagement can benefit. The earlier you begin, the more time there is to build the foundations rather than simply preparing for the ceremony.

Do you offer premarital counseling in Brentwood and Nashville TN?

Yes. I offer EFT-based premarital counseling throughout Tennessee via secure telehealth, including Brentwood and Nashville. I also incorporate the PREPARE/ENRICH assessment into all premarital work.

What topics does premarital counseling cover?

Premarital counseling with me covers communication styles and conflict patterns, attachment styles and emotional needs, family of origin and the invisible scripts each partner carries, finances and role expectations, children and parenting philosophies, sexuality and physical intimacy, faith and values, cultural and family background differences, and how to maintain connection through life's transitions. The PREPARE/ENRICH assessment identifies which areas need the most attention for your specific relationship.

Is premarital counseling right for us if we come from different cultural or religious backgrounds?

Yes, and it is especially valuable. Intercultural and interfaith couples often carry assumptions about marriage, family, and emotional expression that differ significantly and may not surface until conflict arises. I have particular attunement to multicultural couples, interfaith partnerships, and couples navigating different immigration or family expectations. Understanding these differences before marriage is far easier than trying to navigate them in the middle of conflict.
